✨ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai Dost? (Features)

Kama Cam ek high-performance smart camera app hai jo aapke smartphone ko ek pro-level DSLR mein badal deta hai. Isme aapko milta hai:

  • Makkhan Sliders: Screen par smoothly zoom karne ke liye vertical slider aur brightness set karne ke liye horizontal slider diya gaya hai.
  • Smart Layout Auto-Fit: Har phone ki screen par (chahe notch ho ya punch-hole) layout ekdum perfectly fit baithta hai.
  • Series Wise Saving: Aap alag-alag events ke liye naye series banakar photos ko clean tarike se organize kar sakte hain.
